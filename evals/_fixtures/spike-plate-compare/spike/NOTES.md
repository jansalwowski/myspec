# Plate anonymisation spike

Two treatments of the detected plate region: synthetic gaussian blur, and a real plate crop from a licensed set pasted over it.

The street frames show unredacted plates, so they stay out of the repo. spike/out/ holds one test card per treatment: a 160x90 hatch background with the plate band treated each way.
